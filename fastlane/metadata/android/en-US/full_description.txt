Contact Flow manages the contacts on your phone and moves them in and out of every common file format — fully offline.

<b>Phone workspace</b>
• Search names, aliases, numbers, pinyin and Arabic transliteration
• Give a contact several aliases — nicknames, kunyas, other spellings — stored in the phone's Nickname field
• Filter by account, starred, group, no number or no name; A–Z scroller
• Create, edit, star, group, share as vCard and delete — single or in bulk
• Batch table for hundreds of contacts at once

<b>Every change is safe</b>
• Bulk changes are shown in a Review screen with field-by-field differences
• A backup of every affected contact is saved before anything is written
• Undo any change from the snackbar or from Settings → History; share backups as .vcf
• Deleting from Google or Samsung accounts is clearly warned

<b>Sync from a spreadsheet</b>
• Export with a contact_id column, edit in Excel, import with “Update contacts on this phone”
• Matches by contact_id, then number, then exact name — shared office numbers are never guessed
• Fill empty, Overwrite or Add numbers; conflicts are left for you to decide

<b>Duplicates</b>
• Finds contacts with the same number or name and merges them into the one you keep; the other names become aliases

<b>Reach people</b>
• Call, SMS, WhatsApp and Signal from any contact; copy numbers in international format
• Personalised messages with {first}, {alias}, {last}, {name}, {company} sent one by one through your own apps; the queue resumes if the app is closed
• Contact Flow never sends anything by itself

<b>Files</b>
• Import and export CSV, vCard, Excel, Numbers, JSON and plain text

<b>Private by design</b>
• Only the contacts permission. No internet, no analytics, no ads.
