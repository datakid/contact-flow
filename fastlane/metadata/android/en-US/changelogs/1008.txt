3.0.0 — calls, tags and number search
• Two builds: Lite (no call log) and Full (adds a Calls tab that suggests numbers you called but never saved)
• Search by number pattern: *36 ends with, 02* starts with, ?, commas, #tags and Lite/Full chips
• Tags on numbers, with a Tags screen, a tag filter and tags in exports
• Country-aware number matching: the same number in two countries is no longer treated as one
• Import: Windows-1256 and Windows-1252 files, quoted-printable vCards, Excel files with prefixed XML, other sheets and leading zeros
• Dedupe now shows a preview and can be undone; Move my data exports contacts, tags and ignored numbers in one file
• Contacts and tags are no longer included in Google Drive backup
